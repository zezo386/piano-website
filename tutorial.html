Controlling the Octave

Octave is the deepness of the notes the lower the octave is the more deep the sound octave is a range between 1 and 10 to change it use the arrow keys up to increase by 2 and down to decrease by 2 right to increase by 1 and left by 1

Controlling the Wave Form

Wave form is the shape of the sound wave it determines the smoothness of the sound there are 4 types

Triangle

this is the classical wave form where the sound wave goes up and down linearly

Sine

this is the smooth wave form where the sound wave goes up and down smoothly

Square

this is the hard wave where it goes up instantly and stays up for some time then goes down instantly

Saw tooth

this is a wave form where the wave goes up and down linearly multipule times in the same note choose the wave form from the selector in the top of the main page
